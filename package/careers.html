How we work

Our mission is to help everyone achieve financial freedom by making simple, smart, and affordable financial products for everyone.

Since opening our doors 10 years ago, Wealthsimple has been backed by some of the leading names in venture capital and finance, with investment rounds totalling more than $1.1 billion. Today, we administer $70+ billion in assets for our millions of clients. As a team of 1000+ employees, we ship products fast, then continuously improve them. Here, everyone is a maker and an owner: that means rigorous prioritization, no red tape, and interruption-free days to think, create, and innovate.

Benefits at Wealthsimple

Financial

  • Retirement savings matching plan through Wealthsimple for Business
  • Company equity for full-time employees
  • Access to Premium client perks, including personal finance learning and a 2% interest rate on your chequing account
  • Paid parental leave with top up compensation

Flexibility

  • 20 vacation days per year

  • 90 Days Away program (more on that below)

Health

  • Top-tier health benefits
  • Life & disability insurance
  • Generous sick and mental health days
  • Employee resource plan including services like RMT, psychology, and physiotherapy
  • Up to $5,000 per year for mental health supports
  • Gender Affirmation benefits

Well-being

  • Up to $1,500 per year for professional development
  • Up to $1,500 per year for wellness and home office expenses
  • Employee resource groups
  • Social impact hours

Remote-first workforce

Output is more important than face time, so once you have internet access, you can choose your working style — fully remote, in-office at our downtown Toronto location, or a mix of both. And full-time employees have the flexibility to work internationally with our 90 Days Away program.

Onboarding is simple too. We’ll send you your MacBook and any other equipment your role requires. You’ll also have access to a wellness credit from day one, covering other home office essentials you need to make your workspace comfortable and productive.
